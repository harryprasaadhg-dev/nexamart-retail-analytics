
# NexaMart Retail - Stakeholder Requirements

## 1. Project Overview

This document describes the information and reporting requirements of the key stakeholders involved in the NexaMart Retail Business Intelligence and Operations Analytics project.

## 2. Stakeholder Requirements

### 2.1 CEO / Management

**Business needs:**
- Monitor overall business performance.
- Understand total revenue and profitability.
- Compare business performance across stores and sales channels.
- Identify major business risks and opportunities.

**Required reports:**
- Executive KPI dashboard.
- Monthly and yearly sales performance.
- Profitability summary.
- Business performance comparison.

### 2.2 Finance Manager

**Business needs:**
- Monitor revenue, costs and gross profit.
- Analyse profit margins.
- Identify the impact of discounts and returns.
- Compare actual financial performance against targets.
- Identify payment reconciliation differences.

**Required reports:**
- Revenue and profitability report.
- Actual versus target report.
- Payment reconciliation report.
- Returns and refund analysis.

### 2.3 Inventory Manager

**Business needs:**
- Monitor stock availability.
- Identify low-stock and out-of-stock products.
- Analyse inventory movement.
- Monitor inventory turnover.
- Identify products with slow-moving stock.

**Required reports:**
- Stock availability report.
- Inventory ageing report.
- Inventory turnover report.
- Reorder alert report.

### 2.4 Marketing Manager

**Business needs:**
- Identify high-value customers.
- Analyse repeat purchases.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Identify inactive customers.
- Compare customer segments.

**Required reports:**
- Customer segmentation report.
- RFM analysis.
- Repeat purchase report.
- Customer value analysis.

### 2.5 Operations Manager

**Business needs:**
- Monitor delivery performance.
- Identify delayed orders.
- Analyse product returns.
- Evaluate supplier performance.
- Monitor order fulfilment efficiency.

**Required reports:**
- Delivery performance report.
- Return analysis report.
- Supplier performance report.
- Order fulfilment report.

## 3. Expected Business Benefits

- Better business decision-making.
- Improved financial visibility.
- Reduced inventory inefficiencies.
- Better understanding of customer behaviour.
- Improved delivery and operational performance.
- Consistent and reliable management reporting.

## 4. Project Scope

The project will use simulated retail business data and analytical tools including MySQL, Python, Excel and Power BI.

The reports will support management, finance, inventory, marketing and operations analysis.
