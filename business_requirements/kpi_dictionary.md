
# NexaMart Retail - KPI Dictionary

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the key performance indicators used in the NexaMart Retail Business Intelligence and Operations Analytics project.

It provides KPI names, definitions, calculation formulas and business purposes to ensure consistent reporting.

## 2. Sales and Profitability KPIs

### 2.1 Total Revenue

**Definition:** Total sales value generated from eligible completed orders after discounts.

**Formula:** Sum of eligible sales after discounts.

**Business purpose:** Measures overall sales performance.

### 2.2 Gross Profit

**Definition:** Revenue remaining after deducting the cost of goods sold.

**Formula:** Net Sales - Cost of Goods Sold.

**Business purpose:** Measures product-level and overall trading profitability.

### 2.3 Gross Margin %

**Definition:** Percentage of net sales retained as gross profit.

**Formula:** (Gross Profit / Net Sales) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Evaluates profitability and pricing performance.

### 2.4 Average Order Value (AOV)

**Definition:** Average net sales value generated per eligible order.

**Formula:** Net Sales / Number of Eligible Orders.

**Business purpose:** Measures average customer spending per order.

### 2.5 Year-over-Year Revenue Growth %

**Definition:** Percentage change in revenue compared with the same period in the previous year.

**Formula:** ((Current Period Revenue - Previous Year Revenue) / Previous Year Revenue) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Measures business growth over time.

## 3. Inventory KPIs

### 3.1 Stock-out Rate %

**Definition:** Percentage of inventory observations where available stock is zero.

**Formula:** (Out-of-Stock Observations / Total Eligible Inventory Observations) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Identifies product availability problems.

### 3.2 Inventory Turnover Ratio

**Definition:** Measures how frequently inventory is sold and replenished during a period.

**Formula:** Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value.

**Business purpose:** Evaluates inventory efficiency.

### 3.3 Days of Inventory

**Definition:** Estimates how many days current average inventory can support sales at the current consumption rate.

**Formula:** Average Inventory Value / Average Daily Cost of Goods Sold.

**Business purpose:** Helps identify excess or insufficient inventory.

## 4. Customer KPIs

### 4.1 Repeat Purchase Rate %

**Definition:** Percentage of purchasing customers who have placed more than one eligible order.

**Formula:** (Customers with More Than One Order / Total Purchasing Customers) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Measures customer retention and repeat purchasing.

### 4.2 Customer Lifetime Value (Historical)

**Definition:** Historical net sales value generated by a customer over the selected analysis period.

**Formula:** Sum of Customer Net Sales During the Analysis Period.

**Business purpose:** Identifies customers who contribute higher sales value.

## 5. Operations KPIs

### 5.1 Return Rate %

**Definition:** Percentage of sold units that were returned.

**Formula:** (Returned Units / Sold Units) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Identifies product quality and customer satisfaction issues.

### 5.2 On-Time Delivery %

**Definition:** Percentage of eligible delivered orders delivered on or before the promised delivery date.

**Formula:** (On-Time Delivered Orders / Eligible Delivered Orders) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Measures delivery service performance.

### 5.3 Payment Reconciliation Difference

**Definition:** Difference between recorded payment amounts and expected order payment amounts.

**Formula:** Recorded Payment Amount - Expected Payment Amount.

**Business purpose:** Identifies payment discrepancies.

### 5.4 Supplier On-Time Delivery %

**Definition:** Percentage of eligible purchase orders received on or before the promised receipt date.

**Formula:** (On-Time Purchase Order Receipts / Eligible Purchase Order Receipts) * 100.

**Business purpose:** Evaluates supplier delivery reliability.

## 6. Reporting Rules

- Cancelled orders must not be included in completed sales.
- Discounts must be accounted for when calculating net sales.
- Refunds for returned items must be handled consistently to avoid double-counting.
- Gross profit must use the corresponding cost of goods sold.
- KPI calculations must use consistent reporting periods.
- Missing or invalid records must be identified before reporting.
- Each KPI must have a clearly defined numerator and denominator where applicable.

## 7. Data and Calculation Notes

The project will use simulated retail business data.

KPI calculations will be implemented using MySQL and validated using Excel and Python. Power BI will be used to present the results through interactive dashboards.

Some KPIs may be refined when the final database structure and available fields are confirmed.
