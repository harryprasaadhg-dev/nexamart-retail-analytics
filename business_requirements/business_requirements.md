
# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Introduction

NexaMart is a fictional Indian omnichannel retail business operating through physical stores and online sales channels.

The purpose of this project is to develop an integrated Retail Business Intelligence and Operations Analytics System.

The system will help management analyse business performance, identify operational problems and make data-driven decisions.

## 2. Business Challenges

The business currently faces the following challenges:

- Difficulty monitoring sales and profitability across stores and channels.
- Limited visibility into inventory availability and stock-outs.
- Difficulty identifying high-value and inactive customers.
- Lack of visibility into delivery delays and supplier performance.
- Manual reporting and limited management-level insights.
